## Page
- Master 16:9 = 338.7 × 190.5 mm (1920 × 1080 px). Margins 12.7 mm. 12 columns, gutter 6.3 mm, column 20.3 mm.
- Text column = 4 grid columns (45–60 characters), max 5. 2–3 text columns per page, never one long line.
- Draft column guides: rosé #FDF6F7. Mock-ups keep the real grid: side margin 3.75 %, gutter 1.875 % (gutter = half a margin).
- A4 documents (portrait 210 × 297 mm): margins 18 mm, 4 text columns, gutter 4 mm, column ≈ 40 mm; body 8.5 pt, hyphenated. Titles, tables and images span all 4 columns.
- One idea per page; the title states the point. Footer: "KOKO arhitektid · <deck> · v<N> · DD.MM.YYYY" + page number. Cover shows "Version N · date"; file names carry it: <Name>_v<N>_<YYYY-MM-DD>_<device>.pdf.

## Logo
- Upper right, fixed: 6.1 mm high, 12.7 mm from top and right. Never scaled, recoloured, outlined, boxed.
- Title starts two logo heights below the top margin, never on the logo's line.
- Black on white; white on black or a calm dark photo area. Clear space = 1 logo height.
- Client logo: separate, upper left, same height.
- Partner lockup: KOKO left, partner right, same height, gap 0.85 × logo height, no divider, group ends at right margin. Bureau Veritas ISO 9001 mark = 1.5 × KOKO height, centred; colour on white, B/W on mono pages. Partners: COBE, Motor.
- Full-bleed photo page: no logo; info (project · place · photographer) in a contrasting strip.

## Schemes (diagrams) and hand drawing
Clarity and beauty. Lines 0.5 pt black, cut walls solid black, glass one thin line, depth from one scheme, exactly one focus per scheme. Ground and courtyards as soft filled surfaces (large light dots close to the background colour). Site outside the building in one soft green (#E3EAD5). No text on top of drawn activity (paths, trees, courtyards): label in clear space or in the caption; entrance path kept free of trees; level marks never over trees. 7.5 pt caps labels, Inter figures. For e-mail PDFs use flat tints instead of dot patterns.
Style catalogue (deck letters):
- A Concept sequence: same isometric view, one change per step, 3–5 steps; Estonian pines, each its own shape (straight trunk, flat clumped crown).
- B/C Plan and section: cut black; plan trees as simple one-tone symbols (circle + branch star); section trees as pines, crown only at the top.
- D Programme and time: focus = largest field, darkest colour.
- E Exploded axonometric: floors lifted by equal steps, white slabs with 1 pt black edges, back walls only; programme in the scheme's light colours; one route in the scheme accent through every level with straight vertical jumps; small people in the accent's deep tone; names on one vertical line right.
- F Urban network: isometric city in three Copper Fjord tones (roofs light, faces mid/deep); streets in copper, main road strongest; street names on the asphalt; soft green trees, white cars/buses, people as dark dots.
- G Gesture sketch: one pen, no ruler, layered lines, signed and dated.
- H Sketch collage: pen lines over flat paper pieces in two scheme colours, hatching for shade.
- I Pen axonometric: whole place in black pen 0.1–0.3 mm, hatching on the side away from the sun, repeated windows/trees/waves; only the new building coloured.
- J Sculpted section: cut walls and slabs solid black with varying thickness, tapered piles, surfaces behind in soft grey gradients, one room in a scheme colour, silhouettes.
- K Street elevation: hairline neighbours, heavier outline for the new building, colour only on its ground floor.
- L–O Flat covers: 4–6 flat shapes (arches, circles, blocks), no outlines/shadows, one scheme per cover (Ember, Copper Fjord, Patina & Brick, Pine Dusk); label top left, logo top right, title bottom left, text colour by brightness.

## Entourage · trees (under study)
The drawn KOKO tree system (v3–v5: techniques, directions A/B/C, species rows) is withdrawn. KOKO tree style will be set anew after the study.
Study deck "Puud arhitektuuribüroode joonistel" (28.09.2026): 11 offices (Foster, BIG, COBE, Heatherwick, Herzog & de Meuron, SANAA, Ishigami, Kuma, Siza, Zumthor, Lacaton & Vassal), each with its own original drawings in plan and view, a description and source links. The drawings are the offices' authorial rights; used only for study in the context of the Estonian Academy of Arts, never public, never copied into KOKO work.
Rules that stay: the tree serves the building · one tree style per project, one hand · no text over trees · entrance path free of trees · level marks never over trees · in axonometry a tree behind never covers the house · for real images use licensed tree packs or Tõnu Tunnel photos with his permission and credit.

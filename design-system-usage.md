Use this system for every KOKO arhitektid deck, A4 document, poster, diagram and visual. Pick one format, one colour scheme and one type version per piece of work, and run the checklist at the end before delivery. These rules override generic design defaults.

## Page

- Slides: 16:9 master, 338.7 × 190.5 mm = 1920 × 1080 px. Margins `margin-slide` (12.7 mm), 12 columns of `column-slide`, gutter `gutter-slide` (half a margin).
- A text column is 4 grid columns (45 to 60 characters, never more than 5). Use 2 to 3 text columns per page, never one long line.
- A4 documents (portrait 210 × 297 mm): margins 18 mm, 4 text columns, gutter 4 mm, column about 40 mm, body 8.5 pt, hyphenated. Titles, tables and images span all 4 columns.
- One idea per page; the title states the point. Draft column guides use `rose-guide`; mock-ups keep the real grid (side margin 3.75 %, gutter 1.875 %).
- Put version and date on the cover ("Version N · date") and in every footer: "KOKO arhitektid · <deck> · v<N> · DD.MM.YYYY" plus the page number, set in `comment`. Bump the version on every change. File names: `<Name>_v<N>_<YYYY-MM-DD>_<device>.pdf`.

## Logo

- The logo files `KOKO-logo_black.svg` and `KOKO-logo_white.svg` (viewBox 1042 × 332) are not in this system yet. Until they are added, set the name "KOKO arhitektid" in plain `title` type where a logo belongs and never redraw the mark.
- Place it upper right, `logo-height` high, `margin-slide` from top and right. Never scale, recolour, outline or box it. Clear space is one logo height. The title starts two logo heights below the top margin, never on the logo's line.
- Use black on `white`; white on black or on a calm dark photo area.
- A client logo goes upper left at the same height. A partner lockup puts KOKO left and the partner right at the same height, a gap of 0.85 × logo height, no divider, and the group ends at the right margin. The Bureau Veritas ISO 9001 mark is 1.5 × KOKO height, centred, in colour on white and black-and-white on mono pages. Partners: COBE, Motor.
- A full-bleed photo page has no logo; its info (project, place, photographer) sits in a contrasting strip.

## Type

- Choose one version per piece of work and never mix them. A: Gotham for print, InDesign and competitions. B: Montserrat for text and Inter for figures, for AI decks, web and screens (`sans`, `figures`). Left aligned.
- Roles: `title` Bold in `ink`; `subtitle` Regular in `grey-700`; `lead` Regular in `ink`; `body` Regular in `ink`; `comment` for comments, links, footers and captions in `grey-500`; `label` in capitals with +0.15 em tracking in `grey-500`; `key-figure` Inter Semibold in `ink`. Sizes are given in px for a 1920 px slide (2 px per pt). Body text is never larger on text pages.
- Captions and photo credits sit under the photo, left, in `comment`.
- Text on colour follows brightness: light colours take the scheme's deepest tone, middle tones take black, dark colours take white; use white on black or a dark photo.
- A comment block has a step-1 tint background and step-4 text of the same hue. Highlight at most one phrase per paragraph.

## Colour

- The core is `white` (about 70 %), `ink` (about 14 %), `grey-700`, `grey-500`, `grey-050`, the muted scales (about 7 %) and `red` (about 1 %).
- Use `red` at most once per page, as one thin full-height column or rule, or as the one word or number to find first. Never use it as a fill, block, heading or background. Diagrams use the scheme accent, not red.
- Muted scales run step 1 to 5, light to deep: `agave`, `apricot`, `stone`, `fjord`, `sand`, `patina`, `bronze`.
- Five colour schemes remain: `pine-dusk` (01), `ember` (04), `marble-oak` (05), `copper-fjord` (08), `patina-brick` (09). The numbers are kept; 02, 03, 06, 07 and 10 were retired on 28.09.2026. Use one scheme per deck, each of 10 colours (5 base and a partner tone for each). Page share, ground to accent: 30 13 11 7 10 6 9 5 5 4. Show a scheme as stripes plus large swatches on one page. The swatch file is `KOKO-colour-schemes.ase`.

## Schemes (diagrams) and hand drawing

- Aim for clarity and beauty. Draw lines at 0.5 pt black, cut walls solid black, glass as one thin line, depth from one scheme and exactly one focus per scheme.
- Ground and courtyards are soft filled surfaces (large light dots close to the background colour); in e-mail PDFs use flat tints instead of dots. The site outside the building is `site-green`.
- Never put text on drawn activity (paths, trees, courtyards): label in clear space or in the caption. Keep the entrance path free of trees and level marks off trees. Labels are 7.5 pt capitals, figures in Inter.
- Style catalogue by deck letter: A concept sequence (same isometric view, one change per step, 3 to 5 steps) · B/C plan and section (cut black; plan trees as a circle with a branch star, section trees as pines with the crown only at the top) · D programme and time (focus is the largest field in the darkest colour) · E exploded axonometric (floors lifted by equal steps, white slabs with 1 pt black edges, one route in the scheme accent through every level, names on one vertical line at right) · F urban network (isometric city in three Copper Fjord tones, streets in copper, main road strongest) · G gesture sketch (one pen, no ruler, signed and dated) · H sketch collage (pen lines over flat paper pieces in two scheme colours) · I pen axonometric (black pen 0.1 to 0.3 mm, only the new building coloured) · J sculpted section (cut walls solid black with varying thickness, one room in a scheme colour) · K street elevation (hairline neighbours, heavier outline for the new building, colour only on its ground floor) · L–O flat covers (4 to 6 flat shapes, no outlines or shadows, one scheme per cover; label top left, logo top right, title bottom left).

## Trees and entourage

- The KOKO tree style is under study and will be set anew; the earlier drawn tree system is withdrawn. Do not copy other offices' drawings into KOKO work: they are used only for study at the Estonian Academy of Arts.
- Rules that stay: the tree serves the building; one tree style and one hand per project; no text over trees; the entrance path is free of trees; level marks never sit over trees; in axonometry a tree behind never covers the house. ## Photos

- Full page: no logo, an info strip. Photo with text: half and half on the grid. Two or three photos: a general view and a detail with horizons aligned. Series: the same crop logic and horizon height.
- Always credit the photographer, for example "Photo: Tõnu Tunnel" for Villa Lohusalu.

## Logo motion

- Animations live only in the viewer `koko-logo-motion.html`, never in the deck. PDFs show the last frame: link logos to the motion page, or use PPTX with MP4, or HTML. Each animation has its own tempo (waits, snaps, overshoot, settle), never even or mechanical.

Logo position, size and title below it · one type version · roles and sizes · lines of 45 to 60 characters · `red` at most once · one colour scheme · diagrams with thin lines, one focus and no text over drawn activity · one tree style per project · photos credited, horizons aligned, full-bleed without logo · footer and page numbers · nothing overflows on the smallest target screen.
